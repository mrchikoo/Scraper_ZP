    2Gmail connection

    Your emails are sent from your own Gmail account. Gmail needs a special 16-letter code for this, called an App Password. Your normal Gmail password will not work.

    How do I get an App Password? (takes about 3 minutes)
    1. Turn on 2-Step Verification. Open myaccount.google.com in your browser and sign in. Click Security. Under "How you sign in to Google", click 2-Step Verification and follow the steps (Google will text a code to your phone). If it already says "On", you can move to the next step.
    2. Open App passwords. Go to Google Account → Security → App passwords. Tip: if you cannot find it, type "App passwords" in the search box at the top of your Google Account page.
    3. Give it a name. Type any name you like, for example Lead Outreach, then click Create.
    4. Copy the 16-character code. Google shows a yellow box with a code like abcd efgh ijkl mnop. Copy it (spaces do not matter) and paste it into the App Password box above. You will not be able to see this code again, but you can always make a new one.
    5. Press "Test connection". If you see a green message, you are done.

    Your App Password is kept only on this computer. It is never shown again here, and it is only used to send your emails through Gmail.
